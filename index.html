HOW TO PLAY

Keep moving, dodge the obstacles, and reach the finish. Timing is everything. So is trying again.
SPACEJump or activate an orb. W, Up, click, and tap work too. Hold for flight, wave movement, and longer robot jumps.
A / DMove in platformer levels. Left/Right or the touch arrows work too.
P / ESCP pauses or resumes. Esc pauses, then exits from the pause screen.
RRestart the current run.
C / XPlace or remove the latest practice checkpoint.
HOLDActivate a dash orb and hold to keep dashing; release to stop.
Practice makes progress.

Practice mode has automatic and manual checkpoints. Platformer levels can also contain their own checkpoint flags. Finish an original campaign level in normal mode to earn stars or moons and orbs.

Original levels, visuals, and synthesized audio. All play and editing happen on your device.
